# English — IELTS & alternatives

## Why this matters

Universities must satisfy themselves (and, in the UK, UKVI) that you can study in English. Two routes:

1. **A test score** (IELTS / TOEFL / PTE / Duolingo / CAEL)
2. **An MOI waiver** — proof your degree was taught in English

---

## ✅ Decision: IELTS Academic

{% hint style="success" %}
**Settled 2026-10-13.** Sit IELTS Academic (not General Training, not DET).

**Why not MOI?** MOI is **refused outright at 9 of 30 UK universities** — including Exeter, Cardiff, Lancaster, Loughborough, Coventry, Aberdeen, UWE Bristol, Teesside, and Middlesex. Also refused at Carleton (Canada) and effectively at Concordia. One ~₹18,000 test removes the variable everywhere.
{% endhint %}

---

## IELTS Academic — what it is

Four sections, each scored 0–9, then averaged:

| Section       | Duration    | What it tests                               |
| ------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Listening** | ~30 min     | 4 recordings, 40 questions                  |
| **Reading**   | 60 min      | 3 long texts, 40 questions                  |
| **Writing**   | 60 min      | 150-word chart description + 250-word essay  |
| **Speaking**  | 11–14 min   | Face-to-face interview                       |

**Overall = average of four, rounded to nearest half-band.** Cost: ~₹17,000–18,000. Validity: **2 years.**

{% hint style="danger" %}
**IELTS General Training is a different test.** You need **Academic.**
{% endhint %}

**Book November–December 2026.** Late enough to stay valid through 2028 if you defer. Early enough for January–March deadlines. Results in ~3–13 days.

**Target: 7.0 overall, no band below 6.0.** 6.5 is the acceptable fallback.

## The alternatives

| Test                | Scale  | Notes                                                      |
| ------------------- | ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| **IELTS Academic**  | 0–9    | Most universally accepted; UK's default                    |
| **TOEFL iBT**       | 0–120  | Strong US acceptance; UK accepts it too                    |
| **PTE Academic**    | 10–90  | Computer-scored, fast results (~48h)                       |
| **Duolingo (DET)**  | 10–160 | Cheapest (~\$65), at home, but **not accepted everywhere** |
| **CAEL**            | 10–90  | Canada-specific                                            |

{% hint style="warning" %}
Each university sets its own minimum and its own list of _which_ tests it accepts. Duolingo is accepted by some (Calgary, Massey) and refused by others.
{% endhint %}

## MOI — who accepts and refuses it

| Outcome       | Count  | Examples                                                             |
| ------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ✅ Accepted   | 4      | Abertay, Edinburgh Napier, Gloucestershire, Surrey                   |
| ❌ Refused    | **9**  | Exeter, Cardiff, Lancaster, Loughborough, Coventry, Aberdeen, UWE, Teesside, Middlesex |

{% hint style="danger" %}

{% endhint %}
