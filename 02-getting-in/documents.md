## Part A — University admission documents

These go to the **university**, not the visa office. Most UK universities use their own portal; Canada uses individual portals too; NZ uses direct application.

| # | Document | Status | Your action | When needed |
|---|----------|--------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | **IELTS Academic** | ⏳ Not yet taken | Book Nov–Dec 2026. Target 7.0 overall, min 6.5 per band. ~₹18,000 | Before any application |
| 2 | **Transcripts (all 8 semesters)** | ⏳ Request from BVM | Ask BVM exam cell for sealed consolidated transcript + semester-wise marksheets | Oct–Nov 2026 |
| 3 | **Degree certificate / provisional** | ✅ Should have | Collect from BVM if not already in hand | With applications |
| 4 | **2 × Letters of Recommendation** | ⏳ Line up referees | 1 academic (BVM faculty who supervised you) + 1 professional (Inventyv manager). Referees submit directly via portal link. | Dec 2026 – Jan 2027 |
| 5 | **Statement of Purpose (SOP)** | ⏳ Draft needed | Evidence-led: CVEs, NASA VDP, CERT-In Hall of Fame, VAPT→engineer career arc. 500–1,000 words. **Tailor per university.** | Per application |
| 6 | **CV / résumé** | ⏳ Draft needed | 1–2 pages. Include CVE table, certifications (CRTA, Google), work experience. | Per application |
| 7 | **Passport copy** | ✅ Valid | Scan bio page | With applications |
| 8 | **Application fee** | 💰 Budget | £0–£75 per UK university. CAD $100–$125 per Canadian university. NZD $0–$50. Budget for ~6 apps. | Per application |
| 9 | **Portfolio / evidence of work** | ✅ Optional | NASA Letter of Recognition, CVE records, CERT-In screenshot — attach as supplementary evidence where portals allow | Per application |

{% hint style="warning" %}
**WES / credential evaluation:** Not needed. UK, Canada, and NZ universities for cyber MScs evaluate your BVM transcript internally. Don't waste ₹25,000+ on WES.
{% endhint %}

{% hint style="warning" %}
**GRE:** Not needed. None of your target cyber MSc programs require it.
{% endhint %}

### B1. UK Student Route visa checklist

| # | Document | Details | Your case |
|---|----------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | **CAS (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies)** | Electronic reference number from your university after you accept an unconditional offer and pay deposit. Contains your course, fees, and qualification details. | ⏳ University issues this — expect May–Jul 2027 |
| 2 | **Valid passport** | At least 1 blank page. Must be valid beyond course end date. | ✅ Already valid |
| 3 | **IELTS score** | As stated on CAS. Most UK unis accept the standard IELTS Academic (not "for UKVI") for degree-level courses. | ⏳ Take Nov–Dec 2026 |
| 4 | **TB test certificate** | **Mandatory for India.** Chest X-ray from a UKVI-approved clinic. Valid 6 months. | ⏳ Book Jun–Jul 2027. Nearest clinics: Ahmedabad (IOM), Mumbai |
| 5 | **Financial evidence (28-day rule)** | Show: unpaid tuition (per CAS) + maintenance. Funds held for **28 consecutive days**, closing balance within **31 days** of application submission. | ⏳ Start parking funds ~May 2027 |
| 6 | **IHS payment** | Immigration Health Surcharge. Student rate: **£776/year** (~₹80K for 1-year MSc). Paid online before visa appointment. IHS reference number needed. | ⏳ Pay when applying |
| 7 | **ATAS certificate** | **See critical note below.** Apply via GOV.UK. Free. Takes 30+ working days. | ⏳ Apply as soon as you have unconditional offer (Apr–May 2027) |
| 8 | **Visa application fee** | **£490** (Student route from outside UK). Paid online. | ⏳ Pay when applying |
| 9 | **Biometrics** | Fingerprints + photo at VFS Global centre (Ahmedabad/Mumbai). Book after submitting online application. | ⏳ Jun–Jul 2027 |
| 10 | **Academic transcripts & degree cert** | Originals of whatever is listed under "Evidence Provided" on your CAS. | ✅ Same docs from admission |
| 11 | **Parental consent** | ❌ Not needed — you'll be 22 at application | ❌ |
| 12 | **Birth certificate** | Only if using parents' bank account for funds. Proves relationship. | ⏳ Keep ready if using parents' account |
| 13 | **Parent's consent + relationship proof** | If funds are in parent's account: signed letter from parent + birth certificate. | ⏳ If applicable |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**⚠️ ATAS — CORRECTION from earlier version**

Cybersecurity MSc programmes are typically classified under **CAH11-01-01 (Computer Science)** or **CAH11-01-03 (Information Systems)** — both are listed in **Appendix ATAS**. This means ATAS clearance **is likely required** for your courses as an Indian national.

**What this means for you:**
- Your CAS will explicitly state whether ATAS is needed — **check this first**
- If required, apply via [GOV.UK ATAS portal](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/academic-technology-approval-scheme) — it's **free**
- Processing takes **30+ working days (6+ weeks)** — apply early
- ATAS certificate is valid for **6 months**
- EU/EEA/AU/CA/NZ/US nationals are exempt — **Indian nationals are NOT exempt**

This was previously marked as "Not required" in an earlier version of this guide. That was incorrect. The correct answer is: **check your CAS, but plan for it.**
{% endhint %}

| Location | Per month | 9-month total | Applies to applications... |
|----------|-----------|---------------|---------------------------|
| **Outside London** | £1,203 | **£10,827** | Submitted on/after 30 Nov 2026 |
| **London** | £1,570 | **£14,130** | Submitted on/after 30 Nov 2026 |

**Your total financial requirement (UK):**
Unpaid tuition on CAS + maintenance (above) + IHS (£776)

{% tab title="🇨🇦 Canada — Study Permit" %}

### B2. Canada Study Permit checklist

{% hint style="info" %}
**SDS is discontinued.** All Indian applicants now go through the standard study permit stream. Master's students at public DLIs are **exempt from the PAL requirement.**
{% endhint %}

| # | Document | Details | Your case |
|---|----------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | **Letter of Acceptance (LOA)** | Official unconditional acceptance from a DLI (Designated Learning Institution). Must include DLI number. | ⏳ After admission offer |
| 2 | **Valid passport** | Must be valid for entire duration of intended stay. | ✅ Already valid |
| 3 | **IMM 1294** | Application for Study Permit Made Outside Canada. Completed online via IRCC portal. | ⏳ Fill when applying |
| 4 | **IMM 5707** | Family Information Form. Details about parents, siblings, marital status. | ⏳ Fill when applying |
| 5 | **Digital photograph** | Meeting IRCC photo specifications (35mm × 45mm, white background). | ⏳ Get taken at photo studio |
| 6 | **IELTS Academic score** | Sent directly to university (for admission). Copy uploaded for study permit. | ⏳ Take Nov–Dec 2026 |
| 7 | **Financial proof** | See breakdown below. Must cover tuition + living + travel. | ⏳ Arrange by application time |
| 8 | **GIC (Guaranteed Investment Certificate)** | Strongly recommended though not mandatory post-SDS. ~CAD $20,635+ from a participating bank (Scotiabank, CIBC, SBI Canada, ICICI Bank Canada). | ⏳ Purchase after getting LOA |
| 9 | **Statement of Purpose / Study Plan** | Why this program, why Canada, career plans. Critical for standard stream processing. | ⏳ Draft separately from uni SOP |
| 10 | **Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)** | From Passport Seva Kendra (PSK) or Regional Passport Office. Valid 6 months. | ⏳ Apply 2–3 months before visa application |
| 11 | **Medical exam (IME)** | Upfront medical via IRCC-panel physician in India. Do it proactively — speeds up processing. | ⏳ Book after submitting application |
| 12 | **Biometrics** | Fingerprints + photo at VFS Global (Ahmedabad/Mumbai). CAD $85 fee. | ⏳ After receiving Biometric Instruction Letter |
| 13 | **Study permit fee** | **CAD $150**. Paid online. | ⏳ Pay when applying |
| 14 | **Academic transcripts & degree** | All marksheets (Class 10 onward recommended), degree certificate, provisional cert. | ✅ Same docs from admission |
| 15 | **Sponsor documentation** | If parents are sponsoring: their bank statements (4–6 months), ITR (2–3 years), employment/salary proof, affidavit of support. | ⏳ Collect from parents |
| 16 | **PAL (Provincial Attestation Letter)** | ❌ **Exempt** — Master's students at public DLIs don't need this. | ❌ Not needed |
| 17 | **CAQ (if Quebec)** | Only if studying in Quebec (e.g., Concordia). Quebec Acceptance Certificate. ~CAD $120. | ⏳ Only if Concordia/Quebec |

#### Canada financial proof breakdown

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| **Living costs** | **CAD $23,448** (since Sep 2026) |
| **First-year tuition** | As per LOA (varies: CAD $27K–$55K) |
| **Return travel** | ~CAD $2,500–$3,000 |
| **Total minimum** | Tuition + $23,448 + travel |

**Acceptable evidence:**
- GIC (locks CAD $20,635+ in Canadian bank — strongest proof)
- Education loan sanction letter from RBI-approved bank
- Bank statements (4–6 months history, no sudden unexplained deposits)
- Scholarship/funding award letters
- Parent/sponsor documentation (ITR, salary slips, bank statements)

{% hint style="info" %}
**💡 Your case:** Get a GIC even though SDS is gone — it remains the cleanest, most officer-friendly proof of liquid funds. Combine with education loan sanction letter.
{% endhint %}

{% tab title="🇳🇿 New Zealand — Fee-Paying Student Visa" %}

### B3. New Zealand Student Visa checklist

| # | Document | Details | Your case |
|---|----------|---------|-----------|
| 1 | **Offer of Place** | Official acceptance letter from an NZQA-approved institution. Must state course duration and total tuition. | ⏳ After admission offer |
| 2 | **Tuition fee receipt** | Proof of payment for first year or as specified in offer. | ⏳ Pay after accepting offer |
| 3 | **Valid passport** | Must be valid for at least **3 months past planned departure date** from NZ. | ✅ Already valid |
| 4 | **IELTS Academic score** | As required by institution (typically 6.5+ overall for PG). | ⏳ Take Nov–Dec 2026 |
| 5 | **Financial proof — living costs** | **NZD $20,000/year** for living expenses. Separate from tuition and travel. | ⏳ Arrange by application time |
| 6 | **Financial proof — return travel** | Prepaid return ticket **or** ~NZD $2,000 allocated for outward travel. | ⏳ Budget for this |
| 7 | **INZ 1014 (Financial Undertaking)** | Sponsorship/undertaking form if parents are funding you. Parents sign. | ⏳ Download from INZ website, get parents to sign |
| 8 | **Sponsor's financial documents** | Parent's bank statements (3–6 months), ITR, employment proof. | ⏳ Collect from parents |
| 9 | **Medical certificate + chest X-ray** | Mandatory for stays **>6 months** (your MSc = 12–18 months). Must be done by an **INZ-designated panel physician** in India. | ⏳ Book after submitting application |
| 10 | **Police Clearance Certificate (PCC)** | Required if total NZ stay will be **≥24 months** (including past visits). From Passport Seva Kendra. Must be <6 months old. | ⏳ Likely needed if 18-month programme |
| 11 | **Academic transcripts & degree** | Marksheets, degree certificate. | ✅ Same docs from admission |
| 12 | **Passport-sized photographs** | Recent, meeting INZ identity specifications. | ⏳ Get taken |
| 13 | **Health & travel insurance** | **Mandatory.** Must cover full duration of studies. Many NZ unis offer group insurance; otherwise buy separately. | ⏳ Arrange after accepting offer |
| 14 | **Visa application fee** | ~**NZD $375** (online application). | ⏳ Pay when applying |
| 15 | **Accommodation proof** | Evidence of where you'll live (uni halls booking, homestay arrangement). If pre-paid, can reduce $20K living cost requirement. | ⏳ After accepting offer |

#### NZ financial proof breakdown

| Component | Amount |
|-----------|--------|
| **Living costs** | **NZD $20,000/year** |
| **Return travel** | Prepaid ticket or ~NZD $2,000 |
| **Tuition** | Paid to institution directly |
| **Total minimum** | $20,000 + travel (tuition shown via receipt) |

{% hint style="info" %}
**💡 Your case:** Education loan sanction letter is accepted. Bank statements should show 3–6 months of stable history. INZ scrutinises sudden large deposits — if using a loan, get the sanction letter early so funds have time to settle.
{% endhint %}

## Part D — Document preparation timeline

- Book IELTS (Nov or Dec 2026 slot)

- Confirm you have degree certificate / provisional in hand
- Ask 2 referees (1 BVM faculty, 1 Inventyv manager) if they'll write LORs

{% step %}
### Nov – Dec 2026

- **Sit IELTS**
- Finalise SOP and CV drafts
- Start submitting applications (Abertay, Sheffield Hallam already open)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Jan – Mar 2027

- Submit remaining applications (SFU deadline **19 Jan**, Concordia/Carleton/Calgary **1 Mar**)
- Apply for education loan pre-approval (compare SBI, Bank of Baroda, Credila, Prodigy Finance)
- Apply for PCC at Passport Seva Kendra (valid 6 months — time it right)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Apr – May 2027

- Start receiving offers
- Accept preferred offer and pay deposit
- **If UK:** Apply for ATAS immediately (takes 6+ weeks)
- **If Canada:** Purchase GIC, apply for CAQ if Quebec
- **If NZ:** Pay tuition, arrange insurance
- Park funds in bank account (start the 28-day clock for UK)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Jun – Jul 2027

- **UK:** Get CAS from university → submit visa online → pay IHS → book TB test (UKVI clinic) → VFS biometrics
- **Canada:** Submit study permit online (IMM 1294 + IMM 5707) → book upfront medical → VFS biometrics
- **NZ:** Submit visa online → book medical + chest X-ray (INZ panel physician) → upload documents
- Collect parent sponsor documents (bank statements, ITR, salary slips) if applicable
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Aug 2027

- Receive visa decision (UK: ~3 weeks, Canada: 4–8 weeks, NZ: ~4 weeks)
- Book flights
- Arrange accommodation
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Sept 2027

🎓 **Start your MSc**
{% endstep %}
