## The Quebec split ✅

!!! warning
    Canada is **not one immigration system** — it's federal + Quebec's own.

    _"Work experience gained in Canada (**including the province of Quebec**) while authorized to work counts."_ ✅ — IRCC CEC eligibility

    _"**You must plan to live outside the province of Quebec.** The province of Quebec selects its own skilled workers."_ ✅ — IRCC Express Entry


**Quebec's own route (Arrima / PEQ) requires French** ✅ — Level 7 oral, Level 5 written.

**Practical effect:** Studying in Montreal is fine. Quebec work experience counts. But you must declare intent to settle **outside Quebec** to use Express Entry. Settling _in_ Quebec means Arrima with French.

!!! info
    **This is why SFU (Vancouver) outranks Concordia (Montreal).**


## Scholarships ✅

!!! danger
    The vault claimed "no India-specific awards exist at any Canadian university." **That's wrong.**


India-specific awards do exist at Canadian universities:

* **Carleton:** Kochar Family Scholarship (India-only, specific faculties)
* **U of Alberta:** India Centenary Scholarship (up to CAD $20,000)
* **U of Toronto:** Karta Catalyst Scholarship (rural India)
* **Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute:** Cross-institutional India-Canada fellowships

However, **none of the cybersecurity-specific programmes on the shortlist** (SFU, Concordia, UNB, Calgary) have India-specific awards. Funding in Canada generally means thesis/research routes with a supervisor.
