# 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

!!! danger
    **Cheapest to study. Hardest to stay.**


## The visa chain ✅

```
Study (1 yr) → Graduate visa (18 months) → find sponsor paying £33,400–41,700
   → Skilled Worker → 5 years → ILR → citizenship after 1 year
```

| Step              | Detail                                                       | Source                              |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| **Student visa**  | Standard route; self-employment prohibited ✅                  | gov.uk Appendix Student ST 26.5(a) |
| **Graduate visa** | **18 months** (applications from 1 Jan 2027) ✅               | gov.uk — was 2 years, reduced      |
| **Skilled Worker** | Requires a **licensed sponsor employer**                     | gov.uk Appendix Skilled Worker     |
| Salary floor      | **£41,700** standard · **£33,400** new entrant (under 26) ✅ | SW 8.1 / Table 1                   |
| ILR               | **5 continuous years** on Skilled Worker. **Student time does NOT count.** ✅ | SW 21.2              |
| **Total to PR**   | **~6.5–7+ years**                                            | —                                   |

## What works for you

* **ATAS:** ⚠️ **Likely required.** Cybersecurity MScs are typically classified under CAH11-01-01 (Computer Science) or CAH11-01-03 (Information Systems) — both are in Appendix ATAS. Indian nationals are not exempt. **Check your CAS** — the university must declare if ATAS applies. Apply early: free, but takes **30+ working days**.
* **Your CGPA:** Clears 2:1 at 24/30 universities; first-class at Plymouth and Lancaster.
* **NCSC certification:** The UK's unique advantage — see [NCSC certification](ncsc-certification.md).

## ⚠️ The settlement trap

!!! danger
    | | Amount |
    |---|---|
    | Typical UK graduate cyber salary | £25,000–£35,000 ⚠️ Reddit-sourced, no HESA data |
    | Skilled Worker standard threshold | £41,700 |
    | New-entrant threshold (under 26) | £33,400 |

    Graduate pay sits **at or below** the visa bar. You could get the job and still be forced home.


!!! info
    **Counter-evidence:** Abertay claims its MSc Ethical Hacking grads start at £35,000–£40,000 ⚠️ (marketing copy, not HESA outcome data). If true, that clears the £33,400 new-entrant bar.


## Chevening ❌

Chevening **cannot fund Sept 2027 or Sept 2028 entry.**

The binding rule: you need **2,800 hours of post-graduation work experience** before the application deadline. ✅ Graduating mid-2026, the earliest eligible cycle is **Oct 2028 → Sept 2029 entry**.

!!! danger
    Earlier vault drafts said "degree must be 2 years old." That's wrong — it's work hours, not degree age.


## Self-employment prohibition ✅

!!! warning
    UK student visa holders **cannot be self-employed or engage in business activity** (Appendix Student ST 26.5(a)). This affects bug-bounty and consulting income while studying.
