# NCSC certification

## What it is

The **National Cyber Security Centre** (UK) certifies university degree programmes that meet its standards. An NCSC-certified degree signals to UK employers that your programme was quality-assured for cybersecurity content.

## Why expiry matters

NCSC certification is granted for a **fixed period**. If it expires before you graduate (2028), the badge on your degree loses its meaning.

## ❌ Common traps

!!! danger
    | Trap | Reality |
    |---|---|
    | **Robert Gordon taught MSc** | Expires **30/09/2027**, not 2029. The 2029 row is the **Graduate Apprenticeship** (a route you can't take). |
    | **Strathclyde** | Only the Graduate Apprenticeship is certified. |
    | **Leeds Beckett** | Distance-learning only. |
    | **Portsmouth** | Distance-learning MSc only. |
    | **Teesside** | Already **expired 30/09/2026**. |
