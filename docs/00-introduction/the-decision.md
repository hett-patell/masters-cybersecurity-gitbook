### Why the UK dropped

!!! danger
    | | Amount |
    |---|---|
    | Typical UK graduate cyber salary | £25,000–£35,000 ⚠️ Reddit-sourced |
    | Skilled Worker threshold | £41,700 |
    | New-entrant threshold (under 26) | £33,400 |
    | **Shortfall** | £6,700–£16,700 |

    You could get the job and still be forced home. That's a structural trap, not an effort problem.


### Does QS rank matter for cybersecurity?

* **For a UK cyber job** — barely. Employers screen on Graduate visa, NCSC certification, and demonstrable skill.
* **For returning to India** — yes. QS 1000+ reads as weak; top-200/300 carries weight.
* **For the visa/PR route** — not at all. No ranking requirement in any immigration rule.
