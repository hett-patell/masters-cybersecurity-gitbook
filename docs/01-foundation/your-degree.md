# Degree & equivalency

## How they read your Indian degree

UK universities classify bachelor's degrees into a system your Indian transcript doesn't use:

| UK class                  | Meaning                       | Typical Indian equivalent                          |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| **First**                 | highest                       | ~70%+ / CGPA 7.5+ (varies by institution tier)     |
| **2:1** (upper second)   | the common master's bar       | ~60% / CGPA 6.0–6.5+                              |
| **2:2** (lower second)   | lower bar, some courses accept | ~55% / CGPA 5.5+                                  |
| Third                     | lowest pass                   | below that                                          |

!!! success
    **Your 8.03/10 clears the 2:1 bar at 24 of 30 UK universities checked.** At several — **Plymouth** (1st = CGPA 7.5+) and **Lancaster** (1st = 70–85%) — it lands in their **first-class** band. First class unlocks bigger scholarships.

## ⚠️ Conversion is NOT universal

Every university publishes **its own** India table, and several tier by **institution**, not just grade:

| University      | How it tiers India                                      | Your position                                                           |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lancaster**   | "varies by ranking of awarding University"; 1st = 70–85% ✅ | Clears 2:1 (60–80%). May hit 1st band. Ambiguous for BVM tier.         |
| **UWE Bristol** | NOOSR Section 1 vs 2 institutions                        | BVM's section unclear                                                   |
| **Loughborough** | Differentiates "on the NIRF"                            | BVM is participant, not ranked — which counts?                          |
| **Aberdeen**    | "for State and Central Universities: 60%"                | BVM is neither state nor central                                        |
| **Coventry**    | 55% route only for listed set (Delhi, Mumbai, Anna…)     | BVM not listed; 60% bar applies — you clear it                          |

!!! danger
    **This is the single biggest admissions risk.** Get your band confirmed **in writing** from each university before applying.
