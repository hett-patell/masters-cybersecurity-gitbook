# The job market

!!! danger
    **Graduate employment outcomes are essentially unevidenced** for every country. This section compiles what exists — which is mostly Reddit, marketing copy, and structural reasoning.


=== "🇬🇧 UK"

    **Structural facts:**

    * Graduate cyber salaries: **£25,000–£35,000** ⚠️ Reddit-sourced, no HESA data
    * Skilled Worker threshold: **£41,700** (new entrant: £33,400) ✅
    * Gap: **£6,700–£16,700** between typical pay and visa bar

    **Counter-evidence:** Abertay claims MSc Ethical Hacking grads start at **£35,000–£40,000** ⚠️ (marketing copy on course page, not HESA Graduate Outcomes survey). If true, that clears the £33,400 new-entrant bar.

    **Sheffield Hallam** publishes 91% satisfaction ⚠️ — no salary or destination data.

    !!! info
        _"Canada's problem is competition. The UK's problem is permission."_

=== "🇨🇦 Canada"

    **Structural facts:**

    * 3-year open PGWP = no employer dependency ✅
    * No salary floor for PGWP ✅
    * Ottawa government/defence roles: citizenship-gated ✅

    **Reddit signal:** _"Applied for over 200 jobs but nothing came back. Pay is lower — 75k."_ ⚠️

    **Real advantage:** Even if the first role isn't in security, you can work _anywhere_ while building experience. No permission barrier.

=== "🇳🇿 New Zealand"

    **Structural facts:**

    * Smallest market (~5.7m people)
    * **Senior-skewed** — nearly every advert wants 3–5+ years
    * Green List Tier 1 requires **NZD $70.00/hr ($145,600/yr)** — high for a fresh graduate
    * Police and NZDF accept **Permanent Residents** for most cyber roles ✅

    **Realistic path:** Graduate → junior role → 1–2 years → qualifying role at $145k+ → Straight to Residence

    **Waikato's track record:** Only one named alumnus found — a Junior Software Engineer (at Gallagher), not a security role.
