# Loans

## Indian education loans

Indian banks lend against an admit letter. Key facts:

| | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Collateral** | Usually required above ₹7.5 lakh (varies by bank) |
| **Interest rate** | ~9–12% (floating) |
| **Moratorium** | Course duration + 6–12 months |
| **Repayment** | 5–15 years after moratorium |
| **Tax benefit** | Section 80E — interest deductible for 8 years |
