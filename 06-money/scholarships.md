# Scholarships

## The two kinds — this distinction is everything

| | 🤖 **Automatic** | 🎯 **Competitive** |
|---|---|---|
| How | Assessed from your application | You must apply / be selected |
| Reliability | **Bank on it** | **Treat as upside — budget £0** |
| Budget treatment | Subtract from gross fee | **Never** subtract from gross fee |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Never plan your budget on a competitive award.** When a page says "up to £X", assume £0 until confirmed.
{% endhint %}

## ❌ The Canada scholarship myth

{% hint style="info" %}
The vault originally claimed "no India-specific awards exist at any Canadian university." **Wrong.** India-specific awards exist at Carleton, U of Alberta (up to CAD $20,000), and U of Toronto. However, **none are at the cybersecurity programmes on the shortlist** (SFU, Concordia, UNB, Calgary). For these programmes, there are no India-specific or automatic awards.
{% endhint %}
