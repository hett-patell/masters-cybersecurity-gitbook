# What it costs

## Your budget: ₹50–70 lakh (education loan)

{% hint style="info" %}
Single applicant, no dependants. Loan-funded. This means proof of funds must come from a **GIC** (Canada), **bank statement** (UK/NZ), or **loan sanction letter**.
{% endhint %}

## The net-cost formula

```
Gross international fee
− automatic scholarship you'll actually receive
= NET TUITION

+ living expenses (proof of funds minimum)
+ health surcharge (UK only)
+ visa/application fees
= TOTAL COST
```
