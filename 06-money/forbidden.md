# Scholarships to avoid

## Return-home bonds

{% hint style="danger" %}
Some scholarships **require you to return to your home country** after graduation. Since your goal is settlement, these are traps — they fund the degree but block the objective.
{% endhint %}

| Scholarship | Bond | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Chevening** | Must return home for **2 years** after study | ❌ Directly contradicts settlement goal |
| **Commonwealth Scholarships** | Return-home condition | ❌ Same |
| **Some government-of-India schemes** | Service bonds | ❌ Check terms carefully |

## Chevening — why it doesn't work for you

{% hint style="danger" %}
Even ignoring the return-home bond:

* **Eligibility:** Requires 2,800 hours of work experience after graduation ✅
* **Your timeline:** Graduating mid-2026 → earliest eligible cycle is Oct 2028 (Sept 2029 entry)
* **Competition:** ~65,000 applications for ~1,500 awards
* **Verdict:** Cannot fund Sept 2027 or Sept 2028. And even if it could, the 2-year return bond kills settlement.
{% endhint %}

## Awards that sound automatic but aren't

{% hint style="warning" %}
| University | Award | Trap |
|---|---|---|
| **Abertay** | "£3,000–£7,000" | Only £3,000 is automatic. The extra £4,000 is the Principal's Excellence Scholarship — competitive/discretionary. |
| **Lancaster** | "£10,000 India Scholarship" | Automatic **only for 1st/2:1 entrants**. A 2:2 gets nothing. |
| **Edinburgh** | "India Merit Scholarship £5,000" | List-gated — only invited applicants |
| **Waikato** | "NZD $15,000 VC's Excellence" | Competitive. Don't subtract from budget. |
{% endhint %}
