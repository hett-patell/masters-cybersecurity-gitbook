# The three countries

Three countries, three settlement maths. Same degree, radically different outcomes.

| |  🇬🇧 UK | 🇨🇦 Canada | 🇳🇿 New Zealand |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Study cost** | Cheapest (£14k–£21k net) | Mid (CAD $28k–$55k) | Most expensive (NZD $57k–$97k) |
| **Post-study work** | 18 months | **3 years** | **3 years** |
| **Route to PR** | Skilled Worker (sponsor needed) | Express Entry (points) | **Green List → Straight to Residence** |
| **Realistic PR** | ~6.5–7 years | ~2–3 years | ~1–2 years |
| **Main risk** | Salary bar + sponsorship | CRS competition | Small market |

{% hint style="danger" %}
**Cost is not the problem. Settlement is.**
{% endhint %}
